KJournal is a journal app for recording substance intake and viewing the resulting
effects on an effect timeline.

Features
- Intake log: record substance, route of administration, dose and time.
- Effect timeline: graph of effect intensity over time.
- Live notification: keep the ongoing effect timeline visible as a notification.
- Timeline widget: check the current effect from the home screen.
- Tolerance / interaction information.
- Backup and restore as JSON.

This build adds a Korean UI and fixes notification/timeline bugs.
